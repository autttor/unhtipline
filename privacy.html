What we collect

  • Only what you submit. Your answers stay in your browser until you press Submit on the review screen.
  • No account. No University login, Google sign-in or phone number. An email address is asked for only if you want a reply.
  • No questions about your identity. We don’t ask for your sexual orientation or gender identity.
  • No advertising, tracking or analytics. Every page, script and font on this site loads from this site.
  • Other people. You don’t need to name anyone, and we never publish what students tell us or who it is about.

Who can read a submission

  • A small team of student reviewers. Each has their own account. There is no shared password.
  • Reviewers sign in with a password and a second step (a code from an authenticator app).
  • Every time a submission is opened, assigned, changed or exported, it is logged with who did it and when.
  • If a reviewer has a conflict of interest in a case, they are blocked from seeing it.
  • When someone leaves the team, their access is switched off.

How it is delivered and stored

  • Your submission goes from this page straight to a private database hosted by Supabase. It is not placed in this website’s code and cannot be read by other visitors.
  • Reviewers are sent an email saying that something arrived. That email contains a reference number and the type of request. It does not contain what you wrote, your name or your email address. The email is delivered by Resend.
  • This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. These companies process technical information such as IP addresses in order to work and to limit abuse.
  • You get a reference number as a receipt. It is not a password and cannot be used to open what you sent.

Contacting other people

Choosing “help with a University office” or “an advocate” does not give us permission to contact anyone. Pride Guide asks that as a separate question, the answer defaults to no, and reviewers see your answer on the case.

This is not the University

Nothing you send here is an official University report, and it is not shared with the University automatically. If you want the University to act, use the official routes on the Resources page.
