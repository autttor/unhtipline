Number one: what we can do for you

You ask for help. We start there.

It is the first thing Pride Guide asks. Choose one, or all four.

1

Advocate for me

A student reviewer speaks up for you with an office or staff member, with or without your name. You decide which.
2

Help me with this office

Housing, records, health, financial aid, Title IX and more. We help you work out who to talk to and what to ask for.
3

Support me

A student to talk it through with, someone to come with you to a meeting, or help finding counseling and community.
4

Help me with conflict resolution

A roommate, classmate, group or instructor. Nobody contacts the other person without checking with you first.

Only want something on the record? You can choose “Just document it”, and you can ask us to explain the official University reporting options.

How Pride Report works

It is a starting point, not a verdict. Student reviewers help, document and refer. They don’t investigate, and they don’t decide whether anyone did something wrong.

You choose how much to share

No University login, Google sign-in, email or phone number is required.

Anonymous

No name or contact details. You get a random access code so you can look at your report later.

No name or contact details. Reviewers read your report but can’t reply to you.

Private follow-up

Still no contact details. You and a reviewer exchange messages on this site using your code.

Identified

You choose to share a name and a way to reach you, so a reviewer can follow up directly. You can ask us to be discreet when we do.

An anonymous report can still contain details that identify you, and the companies hosting this site process technical information such as IP addresses. Read the limits of confidentiality.
