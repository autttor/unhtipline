What Pride Report does

It is a starting point, not a verdict. Student reviewers document, support and refer. They don’t investigate, and they don’t decide whether anyone did something wrong.

1

Listen

Describe what happened in your own words, in a guided conversation you control.

2

Inform

See which campus resources and reporting routes fit your situation.

3

Connect

Decide whether you want support, a reply, or help reaching an official office.

4

Notice patterns

De-identified trends help show recurring problems, with small numbers hidden.

You choose how much to share

No University login, Google sign-in, email or phone number is required.

Anonymous

No name or contact details. You get a random access code so you can look at your report later.

Private follow-up

Still no contact details. You and a reviewer exchange messages on this site using your code.

Identified

You choose to share a name and a way to reach you, so a reviewer can follow up directly.

An anonymous report can still contain details that identify you, and the companies hosting this site process technical information such as IP addresses. Read the limits of confidentiality.
