How can we help?

It is the first thing Pride Guide asks after checking that you are safe. Each choice has its own short set of questions, and nearly all of them can be skipped.

I need to report something

Tell us what happened, in your own words, so that it is on record with the student team.

I need help with a University office

Housing, records, financial aid or any process. We help work out who handles it and what to ask.

I need peer support

Someone to listen, or help finding community. Students, not counselors, and not immediate.

I need an advocate

Someone to help you speak up: understanding options, wording a message, or raising a concern.

I need information or resources

Reporting routes, support services and community, with no need to send us anything.

Something else, or not sure

You don’t have to put it in a category.

What to know before you start

Student-run

Reviewers are students. They can listen, explain options and help you prepare. They can’t investigate, discipline anyone, or override the University.

You stay in control

Nothing is sent until you press Submit. Asking for help is never permission for us to contact anyone. We ask about that separately.

Not anonymous by magic

You don’t have to give your name, but what you write may identify you, and hosting companies process IP addresses.

Not the University

Sending something here does not file a University report. Resources lists the official routes.

Read exactly how your information is handled.

In immediate danger?

Call University Public Safety at (203) 932-7070, or call 911. Pride Report is not monitored 24/7 and cannot send help.
