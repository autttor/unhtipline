About & privacy

Who we are and what happens to what you tell us

Plain answers, including the uncomfortable ones. If anything here is unclear, don’t submit until it isn’t.

Project status: prototype. Pride Report is a proposed student initiative and is not yet accepting real reports. Items marked to be decided below must be settled, and reviewed with appropriate University staff, before launch.

Who runs this

Pride Report is an independent, student-led initiative at the University of New Haven. It is not operated, endorsed or monitored by the University, and it is not an official reporting channel.

Student reviewers can read what you submit, offer support, explain your options, and help you reach an official office if you ask. They cannot investigate, discipline anyone, or decide whether something “really happened.”

What we collect

  • Only what you submit. Your answers are assembled in your browser and nothing is sent until you press Submit on the review screen.
  • No account. No University login, Google sign-in, email or phone number is required.
  • No questions about your identity. We don’t ask for your sexual orientation or gender identity. Please share only what you want to.
  • No advertising, tracking pixels or analytics. Every file on this site loads from this site; there are no third-party scripts or fonts.
  • Other people. You don’t need to name anyone. We don’t publish accusations or names.

IdentifiedA name and contact method you chooseEverything above, and a reviewer may contact you directly

Your access code is random, shown once, and stored only in scrambled (hashed) form. We can’t look it up or reset it for you.

Limits of confidentiality

  • “Anonymous” is not a guarantee. What you write may identify you to someone who knows the situation. Leave out details you aren’t comfortable sharing.
  • Hosting providers see technical data. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which records visitor IP addresses for security purposes. The service that stores reports also processes IP addresses in order to work and to limit abuse. Visiting this site is not untraceable.
  • Your device and network. Your browser history, a shared computer, or a network you don’t control may show that you visited. The Quick exit button leaves the site but can’t clear your history.
  • Safety and legal obligations. To be decided before launch: the exact circumstances in which reviewers must tell someone else — for example an immediate threat to someone’s safety — and how this interacts with Title IX and campus safety requirements. Until that protocol is published here, the service will not accept real reports.

Who can read a report

  • Only a small number of designated, trained student reviewers and any approved advisers.
  • Reviewers sign in with two-factor authentication, and every time a report is opened or changed it is recorded in an audit log.
  • Reports are never shared with University officials automatically. If you want help contacting an official office, you ask for it.
  • We may publish de-identified statistics, such as counts by category. Groups smaller than five are hidden so individuals can’t be picked out.

How long reports are kept

To be decided before launch. The working proposal is that reports and their messages are deleted automatically 12 months after submission, and sooner when a reviewer closes and deletes them. The audit log keeps only reference numbers, never report content.

What Pride Report is not

  • Not an emergency service. Nobody watches submissions in real time. In danger, call 911 or University Public Safety at (203) 932-7070.
  • Not an official complaint. Submitting here does not notify the University. See Resources for the official routes.
  • Not counseling, legal advice, or an investigation.
  • Not an AI. Pride Guide is a fixed script. It doesn’t generate advice or assess your report.

The text-message number

If you found us through a text number, that number only sends back a link to this site. It is not a confidential reporting line — please don’t text details of an incident to it.
