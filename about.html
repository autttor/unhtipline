Who runs this

Pride Line is run by students at the University of New Haven. It is independent. It is not affiliated with, operated by, or endorsed by the University, and it does not use the University’s name or marks to suggest otherwise.

What the student team can do

  • Keep a record of what you tell us, if that is all you want.
  • Help with a University office: work out who handles something, explain a process, help word a message, or help you prepare for a meeting.
  • Peer support: listen, and help you find community and established support.
  • Advocacy: help you understand options and speak up. A reviewer contacts someone else only if you give separate, specific permission.
  • Point you to resources and to the official University reporting routes.

What it cannot do

  • It is not an emergency service and is not monitored 24/7. In danger, call University Public Safety at (203) 932-7070, or call 911.
  • It is not an official University report. Sending something here does not notify the University or start any University process.
  • Reviewers are students. They cannot investigate, discipline anyone, give legal advice, represent you legally, guarantee an outcome, or override a University decision.
  • It is not counseling. Peer supporters are not counselors.
  • Pride Guide is not an AI and not a person. It is a fixed script that asks questions. It does not judge or assess what you tell it.
