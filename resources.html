Resources and reporting routes

You can use any of these without sending anything to Pride Report, and sending something to Pride Report doesn’t stop you from using them later.

In immediate danger?

Call University Public Safety at (203) 932-7070, or call 911. Pride Report is not monitored 24/7 and cannot send help.
